Dining

Breakfast, lunch and dinner are prepared by our chefs and served at the table, with a choice of healthy dishes at every meal. The main dining room sits at the heart of the building, full of color and natural light.

Between meals, the country-style kitchen is where residents stop for tea and cookies or a cold drink. Hosting family? Book the private dining room. Feeling under the weather? Meals can be brought to your apartment.

Lunch is on us. Visitors are welcome to a free lunch in the dining room any weekday. Call {{phone}} to reserve.

Activities and outings

Each month our activities coordinator puts together a new calendar with something for every interest and ability.

Out and about

  • Museums, arboretums and historic sites
  • Theater and shows
  • River and harbor cruises
  • Local restaurants, libraries and shopping
  • Scenic bus rides

At home

  • Live musicians and dance instructors
  • Lectures by college professors and writing classes
  • Arts and crafts, storytelling and movies
  • Card and board games in the Pub
  • Visits from live animals

Exercise and wellness

Each resident's health and wellness plan is put together with them, their family and our wellness team. Personal care assistants and activities staff are here day and night.

Classes are planned and supervised: stationary equipment, low-impact aerobics, stretching, strength work and yoga.

  • Health and wellness assessment
  • Help with daily activities
  • Help getting around the apartment and campus
  • Friendly reminders when needed
  • Care coordination with doctors
  • Medication and continence management

Wooded and quiet, close to everything

Windsor Place sits on 16 acres next to protected conservation land, with gardens, courtyards and patios to enjoy. Yet it's minutes from Andover, Burlington, Woburn, Reading and North Reading, and about 20 minutes from downtown Boston via I-93, so family can visit easily.
