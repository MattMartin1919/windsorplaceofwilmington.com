About assisted living

What is assisted living?

Assisted living is a home-like residence that combines a private apartment with personal care for older adults who need help with daily activities but don't need 24-hour skilled nursing.

How is assisted living different from a nursing home?

Assisted living residents are more independent. They might need help with housekeeping, bathing or medication reminders, and they enjoy structured activities, outings and social events. Most are in their 70s and 80s.

How will we know when it's time to move?

There's rarely one moment. Families usually notice missed medications, weight loss, falls, a house that's become too much, or loneliness. We hold regular information sessions, and our relocation team is happy to talk it through. Call to set up a time.

Where can I find more information on assisted living in Massachusetts?

Good sources include the Massachusetts Executive Office of Aging & Independence, the Massachusetts Assisted Living Association (Mass-ALA), the Massachusetts Senior Care Association, and your town's Council on Aging.

Costs and paying

How much does it cost to live at Windsor Place?

Rent is all-inclusive and depends on the apartment and care plan. We suggest adding up what you spend now on housing, utilities, food, upkeep and help at home, then comparing it with our rent. Our relocation team will go through it with you in detail.

Are apartments rented or purchased?

Rented. There's no large up-front purchase and none of the uncertainty of owning real estate.

Is financial help available?

Most residents pay privately through the sale of a home, income, savings or family support. Some use long-term care insurance, Veterans Administration benefits for eligible veterans and spouses, or state programs such as Group Adult Foster Care.

Daily life

How large is Windsor Place?

We're on 16 acres next to protected conservation land, with studio, one-bedroom and two-bedroom apartments looking out on woods or courtyards. The Carlysle provides enhanced safety suites and memory care.

Are apartments furnished?

Apartments come fully equipped, and residents bring their own furniture and personal touches. Trial and respite stays use a furnished apartment.

What amenities are there?

A paneled library, a living room with a large fireplace, a country-style kitchen, fitness area, beauty salon, a pub lounge, private dining room and the main dining room.

Will meals be served in my room?

Meals are served in the main dining room. When needed or requested, they can be served in your apartment. The private dining room is available for small family gatherings.

Is there transportation to appointments and shopping?

Yes, we offer scheduled transportation for residents.

Can I bring my car?

Yes. There's ample parking for residents and guests.

Care and safety

Do residents manage their own medications?

They can. Staff can also remind residents and help make sure they follow the treatment plan their doctor prescribed. Residents in The Carlysle receive closer medication support.

Is a staff member always available?

Yes. Personal assistance is available 24 hours a day, seven days a week, and every apartment has an emergency call system.

How do you keep residents safe?

Staff get to know each resident's routine and notice changes. If something seems wrong, they alert colleagues, family or medical professionals. We also respect privacy: staff don't enter an apartment without an invitation.

Is Windsor Place certified?

Yes. We're certified by the Massachusetts Executive Office of Aging & Independence, meeting state standards for assisted living, including special care for Alzheimer's and related disorders.
