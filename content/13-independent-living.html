The Independence Plan

You get everything that makes Windsor Place a good place to live: a private apartment, meals, the social calendar, housekeeping and transportation. You don't pay for personal care services you don't need.

And if that changes, you can switch to assisted living without moving to a new community.

  • No more home repairs, yard work or maintenance bills
  • Neighbors and friends right down the hall
  • Chef-prepared meals, and someone else does the dishes
  • Trips to museums, theaters and harbor cruises
  • Help a few steps away, day or night
  • Personal emergency call system in every apartment

Freedom with a safety net

Living alone can be quietly risky. A fall can go unnoticed for hours. Our staff respect your privacy and won't enter your apartment uninvited, but they do notice when something changes, and they'll check in or reach out to family if anything seems wrong.
