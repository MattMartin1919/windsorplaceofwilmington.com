Your apartment

Every apartment is rented, so there's no buy-in and no real estate to worry about. They come ready to live in. You bring the furniture, photos and favorite chair.

  • Kitchenette and generous closets
  • Private bathroom designed for safety
  • Wall-to-wall carpeting
  • Your own heat, air conditioning and hot water controls
  • Personal emergency response call system
  • Smoke and carbon monoxide detectors, fire alarms and full sprinklers
  • Your choice of phone and cable TV provider
  • High-speed internet access

Shared spaces and services

The building was designed by an architectural firm with close attention to light, color, texture and sound, so common rooms feel warm rather than institutional.

  • Main dining room with grand piano and table-side service
  • Paneled library with fireplace
  • Living room with a large fireplace
  • The Pub, a cocktail lounge with its own fireplace
  • Country-style kitchen for tea, cookies and a chat
  • Billiards room
  • Private dining room for family gatherings
  • Fitness area
  • Beauty salon with hair and nail services
  • Activities room and entertainment room
  • Patios, courtyards and walking paths
  • Weekly housekeeping and flat linen service
  • Washers and dryers on every floor
  • Ample parking for residents and guests

Technology that stays in the background

Every apartment is wired for the systems that keep residents safe, connected through the building's fiber-optic network. Residents don't notice them until they need them.

  • High-speed internet for video calls with family
  • Personal emergency response call system
  • Medication management and care planning systems
  • Capacity for monitoring and assistive devices

Built to green standards

Windsor Place was designed to LEED standards on a repurposed site close to major roads.

  • Energy-efficient apartment layouts and mechanical systems
  • Green building materials and a well-insulated exterior
  • On-site water infiltration and low-water plantings
  • Outdoor patios and walking paths across the campus
