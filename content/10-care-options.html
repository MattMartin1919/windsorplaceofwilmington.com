How we build a care plan

Before move-in, we sit down with the resident and family. With your permission we also talk to the people who know them best, such as their doctor, pharmacist or visiting nurse.

The goal is simple: the right amount of help, and no more. We want each resident safe and supported while doing as much for themselves as they can and want to.

Plans are reviewed with the family as things change. Extra services can be added for a few weeks after a hospital stay, or for good.

Medication management

Getting medications right is one of the most important things we do. Staff follow Massachusetts assisted living regulations to help residents take what their doctor prescribed, when it was prescribed.

  • Reminders and self-administered medication management (S.A.M.M.) in assisted living plans
  • Closer medication support in memory care
  • We work with a local pharmacy, or use any pharmacy you choose
  • A medication management system built into the community's technology
