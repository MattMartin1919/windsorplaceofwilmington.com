Designed around how memory loss changes a day

The Carlysle has a select number of suites and its own common areas, so residents get to know the same faces. Many suites open straight onto a secure garden, so going outside never needs a plan.

  • Secured entrance and exit
  • Private outdoor patio and garden
  • Caregivers with specialized Alzheimer's and dementia training
  • Staff on duty 24 hours a day
  • Up to two hours of personal care in every 24 hours, as needed
  • A personal wellness care plan for each resident
  • Medication management
  • Limited continence management
  • Meals in a smaller, quieter dining room
  • Housekeeping, laundry and apartment upkeep

Days with purpose

Each day follows a steady rhythm of activities for the body, the hands, the heart and good company. Familiar routines help residents feel settled. Supervised trips to local spots keep the world open to them.

Families are part of the plan. We meet with you before move-in to learn your loved one's history, habits and what brings them joy, and we keep you posted as things change.

Memory care questions

When is it time to consider memory care?

Families often call after wandering, getting lost, leaving the stove on, missed or doubled medications, or when caregiving at home has become exhausting. If safety is a worry, it's time to visit. We'll talk through what you're seeing, honestly.

Is The Carlysle state certified?

Yes. Windsor Place is certified by the Massachusetts Executive Office of Aging & Independence, which includes the standards for special care residences serving people with Alzheimer's and related disorders. View our certificate (PDF).

Can a spouse live nearby?

Because The Carlysle is part of Windsor Place, a spouse can live in an assisted or independent living apartment in the same building and visit any time. Ask us about current availability.
