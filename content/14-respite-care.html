Respite care

Respite stays are typically about 30 days. Your loved one gets a furnished apartment and as much or as little personal care as they need, with our team on site day and night.

Families use respite care when:

  • A caregiver needs rest, or is traveling
  • An older relative can't or won't come along on a trip
  • Someone is recovering and isn't ready to be home alone
  • You'd like to see how assisted living fits before committing

The 30-day trial stay

Not sure about a move? Live here for a month first. There's no commitment.

  • A furnished apartment, ready the day you arrive
  • All meals and activities included
  • An easy move-in, with no long-term contract
  • Time to meet neighbors, try the food and see the care up close
