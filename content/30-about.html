Our story

The Paino family are longtime members of the North Shore business community, with deep ties to the region's medical community. They built Windsor Place on a simple idea: go the extra mile, and do the little things that make a difference.

Because the owners are on site, decisions get made quickly and close to residents. One family put it this way: “I am positive that having proactive owners is what makes this place as lovely as it is!”
