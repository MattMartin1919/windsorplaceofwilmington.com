The comfort of a New England inn, with care down the hall

Windsor Place sits on 16 acres beside conservation land, so most apartments look out on woods or a garden courtyard. Inside there's a paneled library, a living room with a big fireplace, a pub, a beauty salon and a dining room with a grand piano.

Apartments are rented, not bought, so there's no large entrance fee. Choose a studio, one-bedroom or two-bedroom and bring the furniture that makes it home.

Questions families ask first

How do we know it's time for assisted living?

Common signs are missed medications, skipped meals, a fall or near-fall, a home that's getting hard to keep up, or simply loneliness. If you're asking the question, it's worth a visit. Our relocation team will talk it through with you, with no pressure.

Do residents rent or buy their apartment?

Apartments are rented. There's no large buy-in or entrance fee, and no real estate risk.

How much does it cost?

Monthly rent is all-inclusive and depends on the apartment and care plan. Most families find it compares well once they add up the mortgage or rent, utilities, food, upkeep and in-home help they pay today. Call us at {{phone}} and we'll go through the numbers with you.

Can we try it before deciding?

Yes. Our 30-day trial stay comes with a furnished apartment, meals and activities, and no commitment. It's a good way to settle in before deciding on a move.
