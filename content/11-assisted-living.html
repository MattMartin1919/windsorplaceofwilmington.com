What's included

Assisted living at Windsor Place is all-inclusive. One monthly rent covers your apartment, meals, care plan and the life of the community.

  • A private studio, one-bedroom or two-bedroom apartment with kitchenette
  • Three chef-prepared meals a day in the main dining room
  • Help with showers or whirlpool baths
  • Occasional help with dressing, grooming and other daily tasks
  • Medication reminders and self-administered medication management
  • Personal emergency call system in every apartment
  • Weekly housekeeping and flat linen service
  • Heat, air conditioning and hot water you control
  • A full calendar of activities, classes and outings
  • Scheduled transportation to appointments and shopping
  • Caregivers on site 24 hours a day, 7 days a week
  • Washers and dryers on every floor

Assisted Living Plan

Our standard plan includes up to one hour of personal care each day. That covers showers or whirlpool baths, occasional help with activities of daily living, and self-administered medication management.

Need a little more for a while, such as after a hospital stay? Add services at any time, for a short stretch or for good.

Enhanced Care Plan

For residents who need more support, the Enhanced Care Plan includes up to two hours of personal care each day, including help with showers, daily tasks and medications.

We put the plan together with the resident and family, and with your permission, the doctors, pharmacists and nurses who know them. The aim is to keep them safe while protecting as much independence as possible.

Assisted living questions

How is assisted living different from a nursing home?

Assisted living is for people who need some help but not 24-hour skilled nursing. Residents live in their own apartments, come and go, and choose how to spend their day. Most are in their 70s and 80s and want company, good meals, safety and a hand with a few daily tasks.

Can my parent bring their own furniture?

Yes, and we encourage it. Apartments come ready with kitchenette, carpeting, emergency call system and climate controls. Residents bring the furniture and keepsakes that make it home.

Can residents keep their car?

Yes. There's plenty of parking for residents and guests. We also offer scheduled transportation for appointments and shopping.

Is help really available at night?

Yes. Staff are on site around the clock, seven days a week, and every apartment has a personal emergency call system.

How do people pay for assisted living?

Most residents pay privately, often from the sale of a home, savings, income or family help. Some use long-term care insurance or Veterans benefits. We can walk you through the options.
